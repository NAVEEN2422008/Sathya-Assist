RECRUITMENTS

Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology’s resounding success of Campus Recruitment continues year on year. For the batch which passed out recently, more than 497+ eminent recruiters participated in the Campus Recruitment and rolled out 4079 Offers as on date. 94.51% of registered students have been placed already. The institute witnessed an increase in the annual CTC offered to its students as the Average CTC raised to 5.73 LPA. It also recorded its highest CTC of 41.2 LPA this year. Additionally, our students experienced a significant rise in the number of Dream, Super Dream and Elite companies hiring the students. This has set a wonderful platform for the upcoming batches as their recruiters’ base grows every year. The presence of eminent recruiters like Amazon, Adobe, Accenture, ADP, Asian Paints, AtoS, Adani Group, Bank of America, Bosch, Caterpillar, Capgemini, Cognizant, cSoft Tech, Catalog, Cprime, Deloitte, DBS Tech, EPAM, Ernst & Young, Godrej, Forbes Marshall, Johnson & Johnson, Hexaware HCL Tech, HPE, Hyundai Inmobis, Honda R&D, GenPact, Gulf Contracting Company, In Out Contracts LLC, ICICI Bank, IBM, IMEG, Juspay, JP Morgan, JSW, LTIMindtree, Openup Constructions, MotorQ, MuSigma, Mckinsey, Natwest, NielsenIQ, Nokia, Oracle, Optum, Redbus, PwC, BNP Paribas, Philips, Renault Nissan, HashedIn, Visteon, Securin, Siemens, Schneider Electric, Sopra Steria, Sona Comstar, Tredence Analytics, Tech Mahindra, Tata Elxsi, Tekion, Virtusa, Valeo, Mphasis, Vivnovation and Zifo RnD, Zoho, Zavity Aerospace shows the world of opportunities that Sathyabama continues to create for its students. Our collaborative approach in industry-institute relations provided large number of job offers through the Centre of Excellences by HCL Tech, Capgemini, Wipro, Brite Systems and so on. The Digital Nurture programme by Cognizant is another great new initiative that has helped a large number of students nurture their employability skills. There are numerous other companies like LTIMindtree, TCS, PwC and so on have a strong connect with us and giving plethora of opportunities to the students to upskill themselves

Recruitment Process:

Guidelines:

  • All final year students are eligible for Campus placement.
  • Training attendance should be a minimum of 90%.
  • Students will be informed about all campus drives through group emails & class room circulars.
  • Students need to confirm their participation in a recruitment drive through the online portal for the job postings that they are interested for.

Data to Destiny:

Dream Companies:

The companies which offer between 5 LPA and 10 LPA will be considered as Dream Company. One condition to this category is that, if the difference in CTC (Salary – Cost to Company) is between the existing offer and the dream offer is less than a 1.5 LPA, then those students will not be allowed to appear for the ‘dream company’ interview.

Super Dream Companies:

The companies which offer above 10 LPA will be considered as Super Dream companies. The condition to this category is that, if the difference in CTC is between the existing offer and the Super dream offer is less than 3 LPA, then those students will not be allowed for the ‘Super dream company’ interview.

Placement Highlights:

Guest Lecture on Self-Motivation by Ebixcash HR– 18.09.26

The Sathyabama Placement Cell organized a guest lecture on “Self-Motivation” on 18 September 2026 at the Placement Cell Auditorium, Central Library. The programme was delivered by Mr. Pankaj Kumar Sinha, Manager – HR, Ebix Group, and was attended by more than 250 MBA students.

The speaker highlighted the importance of goal setting, positive thinking, perseverance, and continuous self-improvement in achieving personal and professional success. Drawing on industry insights, he encouraged students to build confidence, overcome challenges, and adopt a proactive approach towards their careers.

The programme inspired students to strengthen their determination, embrace a growth-oriented mindset, and prepare themselves to navigate corporate challenges with confidence and resilience.

SAP Hackfest – 22.09.26

Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology hosted the SAP Hackfest on 22 September 2026, witnessing the participation of more than 91 Engineering students. The hackathon provided a platform for students to demonstrate their technical expertise, creativity, and problem-solving abilities through innovative solutions.

A panel of jury members evaluated the participants and monitored their work throughout the event, ensuring a structured and fair assessment of their ideas and implementation. The hackathon encouraged collaborative learning, innovation, and the practical application of technical knowledge to address real-world challenges.

The event fostered a spirit of innovation and technical excellence, enabling students to gain valuable hands-on experience and strengthen their readiness for industry-oriented challenges.
