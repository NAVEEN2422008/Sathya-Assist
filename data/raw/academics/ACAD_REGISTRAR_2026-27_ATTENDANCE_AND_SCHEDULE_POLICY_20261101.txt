OFFICE OF THE REGISTRAR & DEAN ACADEMICS
CIRCULAR: ACADEMIC POLICIES, ATTENDANCE REGULATIONS & WORKING HOURS
Applicable Academic Year: 2026-2027

1. DAILY ACADEMIC SCHEDULE & TRANSPORT WINDOW
- Regular Instructional Hours: 08:45 AM to 03:30 PM on all official working days.
- College Bus Schedule: The institutional bus fleet arrives at campus prior to 08:45 AM and departs promptly at 03:30 PM.
- Extended Coaching & Facility Access: Special coaching buses for Placement Training, Civil Services Academy, and Central Library access depart at 05:00 PM.

2. SEMESTER DURATION
- Each academic semester comprises a minimum of 90 working days (equivalent to 450 instructional contact hours).
- Maximum Permissible Duration: Normal duration for B.E. / B.Tech is 4 years (8 semesters). The maximum permissible period to satisfy degree completion requirements is 8 years (16 semesters).

3. MANDATORY ATTENDANCE REGULATIONS
- 90% Minimum Requirement: A student must secure a minimum of 90% attendance in each registered theory and practical course to be eligible to appear for the University End Semester Examinations.
- Condonation for Medical / Authorized Leave (80% to 89%): Students securing between 80% and 89% overall attendance due to documented medical hospitalization or authorized official university on-duty (OD) representation may be granted condonation upon approval by the Dean and payment of the prescribed institutional condonation fee.
- Below 80% Attendance: Students with less than 80% attendance in any course are strictly barred from appearing for the End Semester Examination in that course and must reregister/repeat the course when offered in subsequent academic sessions.
