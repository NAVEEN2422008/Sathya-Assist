HOSTEL RESIDENCE GUIDELINES, GATE TIMINGS & CAMPUS AMENITIES
Academic Year: 2026-2027

1. HOSTEL RESIDENTIAL BLOCKS
- Sathyabama operates 10 hostel residential blocks comprising 5 Boys Hostels and 5 Girls Hostels with twin, triple, and multi-sharing room options.
- Continuous 24/7 power backup, high-speed Wi-Fi connectivity, and RO purified drinking water systems are maintained across all residential floors.

2. GATE TIMINGS & CURFEW REGULATIONS
- Evening Curfew: All hostel students departing the campus for day outings or permitted local transit must return to their respective residential blocks before 06:30 PM.
- Movement Monitoring: Campus ingress and egress are strictly monitored through biometric scanners and security check posts.
- Outing Permissions: Overnight and weekend leave requests must be submitted digitally in advance through the official student hostel portal.

3. MESS & DINING FACILITIES
- Fully air-conditioned and ventilated dining halls provide comprehensive vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus tailored for South Indian, North Indian, and international students.
- Food delivery personnel (Swiggy, Zomato, etc.) are restricted to designated security drop points at Main Gate 1 and Gate 3 and are prohibited past 08:30 PM. Direct access to hostel blocks is prohibited.

4. HEALTHCARE & EMERGENCY SERVICES
- 24x7 Medical Room: Staffed with residential medical officers on duty within hostel premises.
- Campus Hospital: Direct access to the on-campus 100-bed Sathyabama General Hospital equipped with 24/7 emergency triage, inpatient wards, ICU, diagnostic laboratories, pharmacy, and dedicated university emergency ambulances.

5. CAMPUS AMENITIES & RECREATION
- Laundry Service: Complimentary mechanized washing and pressing services provided twice weekly on designated room rotation schedules.
- Fitness & Recreation: Dedicated gymnasium and fitness centers for boys and girls, indoor badminton courts, table tennis, TV recreation lounges, and campus grooming salons.
- Banking & ATM: Full-service on-campus Indian Bank branch with multiple 24-hour ATMs.
