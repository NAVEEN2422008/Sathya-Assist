Office of the Controller of Examinations (COE)
REGULATIONS: EVALUATION SCHEME, 10-POINT GRADING SYSTEM & REVALUATION RULES
Effective Session: 2026-2027 Academic Year

1. MARKS EVALUATION WEIGHTAGE
The evaluation pattern for all Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) theory courses consists of:
- Continuous Assessment Examinations (CAE): 50% Weightage (2 centralized CAE exams conducted per semester).
- University End Semester Examination (ESE): 50% Weightage.

2. MINIMUM PASSING CRITERIA
- A candidate must secure a minimum of 50% marks in the End Semester University Examination.
- In addition, a minimum aggregate of 50% combining Continuous Assessment (CAE) marks and End Semester Examination marks is strictly required to pass any course.

3. 10-POINT LETTER GRADING SCALE
The performance of a student in a course is evaluated using the following 10-point scale:
- O (Outstanding): Grade Points 10 (Marks Range: 90 - 100)
- A+ (Excellent): Grade Points 9 (Marks Range: 80 - 89)
- A (Very Good): Grade Points 8 (Marks Range: 70 - 79)
- B+ (Good): Grade Points 7 (Marks Range: 60 - 69)
- B (Above Average): Grade Points 6 (Marks Range: 55 - 59)
- C (Average): Grade Points 5 (Marks Range: 50 - 54)
- P (Pass): 50% Minimum Passing Score
- F (Fail): Below 50% Marks
- AB (Absent)

4. ARREAR EXAMINATIONS & REVALUATION PROCEDURE
- Students obtaining 'F' (Fail) or 'AB' (Absent) grades must register for arrear examinations in subsequent semester cycles.
- Revaluation & Answer Script Photocopy: Candidates can apply for a photocopy of their evaluated answer scripts and request revaluation through the Controller of Examinations within 10 days from the date of declaration of results on the official results portal (result.sathyabama.ac.in).
