CIRCULAR: END SEMESTER EXAMINATIONS - NOVEMBER/DECEMBER 2025
Applicable to: All UG & PG Students (Full Time)

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA & HALL TICKETS
All students must secure a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to appear for the End Semester Examinations. Hall tickets will be released on the student portal on 2025-11-15 (15 November 2025). Students with attendance between 65% and 74% due to medical reasons must submit their medical certificates to the Dean Office by 2025-11-12.

2. EXAMINATION TIMINGS
Morning Session: 09:30 AM to 12:30 PM (Students must report by 09:00 AM)
Afternoon Session: 01:30 PM to 04:30 PM (Students must report by 01:00 PM)
Late entry after 09:45 AM or 01:45 PM is strictly prohibited.

3. DRESS CODE & PROHIBITED ITEMS
- Proper formal attire with mandatory College ID Card is required.
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, digital calculators, and unauthorized paper chits are strictly banned inside examination halls. Possession of these items will attract immediate Malpractice (MP) booking under Clause 14.2 of University Regulations.

4. ARREAR REGISTRATION DEADLINE
Last date for regular arrear fee payment without fine is 2025-11-18. With a penal fine of Rs. 500, registrations will remain open until 2025-11-22.
