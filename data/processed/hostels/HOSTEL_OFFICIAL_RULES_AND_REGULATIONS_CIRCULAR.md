         Hostel inmates are advised not to keep cash or valuables in their rooms; 
                                                                                  
         Hence complaint about loss or theft about such costly items is discouraged
         Smoking, Alcohol & Narcotic consumption is strictly prohibited inside the
                                                                                  
         Hostel premises; Strict action will be taken against offenders

         encouraged

         8:30 pm to 10:00 pm to get permission letters signed in the Warden’s office

         audible outside the room

         circumstances; Hostel inmates are responsible for the care of furniture and

         expulsion from hostel immediately without refund                         
         Hostel fee once paid is non refunded / non transferable

                    Penalty for Damage  to Hostel Property                        
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
         Fine will be levied for disfiguring of doors, glass panels, furniture and walls
         of rooms from all the inmates of the room in addition to the amount      
                                                                                  
         needed for repairing and repainting                                      
                                                                                  
         Collective fine will be collected from the inmates of the respective wings /
         floors for disfiguring Veranda’s in addition to the amount needed for    
                                                                                  
         repairing                                                                
         Collective fine will be collected from the inmates of respective blocks for
                                                                                  
         disfiguring the common rooms or damaging the property of hostel blocks,  
                                                                                  
         in addition to the replacement cost                                      
         Hostel Furniture shall not be removed from one room to another under any 
                                                                                  
         circumstances; Hostel inmates are responsible for the care of furniture and
         fittings in their respective rooms
