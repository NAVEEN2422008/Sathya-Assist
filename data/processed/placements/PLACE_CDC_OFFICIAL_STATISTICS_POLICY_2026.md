Data to Destiny:
Dream Companies:
The companies which offer between 5 LPA and 10 LPA will be considered as Dream Company. One condition to this category is that, if the difference in CTC (Salary – Cost to Company) is between the existing offer and the dream offer is less than a 1.5 LPA, then those students will not be allowed to appear for the ‘dream company’ interview.
Super Dream Companies:
The companies which offer above 10 LPA will be considered as Super Dream companies. The condition to this category is that, if the difference in CTC is between the existing offer and the Super dream offer is less than 3 LPA, then those students will not be allowed for the ‘Super dream company’ interview.

Placement Highlights:
Guest Lecture on Self-Motivation by Ebixcash HR– 18.09.26
The Sathyabama Placement Cell organized a guest lecture on “Self-Motivation” on 18 September 2026 at the Placement Cell Auditorium, Central Library. The programme was delivered by Mr. Pankaj Kumar Sinha, Manager – HR, Ebix Group, and was attended by more than 250 MBA students.
The speaker highlighted the importance of goal setting, positive thinking, perseverance, and continuous self-improvement in achieving personal and professional success. Drawing on industry insights, he encouraged students to build confidence, overcome challenges, and adopt a proactive approach towards their careers.
The programme inspired students to strengthen their determination, embrace a growth-oriented mindset, and prepare themselves to navigate corporate challenges with confidence and resilience.
SAP Hackfest – 22.09.26
A panel of jury members evaluated the participants and monitored their work throughout the event, ensuring a structured and fair assessment of their ideas and implementation. The hackathon encouraged collaborative learning, innovation, and the practical application of technical knowledge to address real-world challenges.
The event fostered a spirit of innovation and technical excellence, enabling students to gain valuable hands-on experience and strengthen their readiness for industry-oriented challenges.
