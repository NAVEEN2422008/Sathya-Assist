# Online Certificate & Transcript Fee Structure

Source: Sathyabama FEE Portal (https://feeportal.sathyabama.ac.in/account/Certificate)

## Prescribed Fee Schedule

| Sl. No. | Document / Service | Official Amount |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1. | Semester Mark / Grade Sheet for all courses | Rs. 400/- per semester |
| 2. | Consolidated Mark / Grade Sheet for all courses | Rs. 800/- |
| 3. | Speed Post Postal Charges (within India) | Rs. 200/- |

## Official Processing Guidelines & Dispatch Policies
1. Turnaround Time: Maximum of 15 working days from the date of receipt of the application form at the Controller of Examinations office.
2. Degree & Transfer Certificates: Transcripts will NOT be issued for Transfer Certificates and Degree Certificates.
3. Attestation Rules: Attestation of grade sheets is NOT conducted by the COE office; attestation is reserved for original Transfer Certificates and Degree Certificates.
4. Postal Dispatch: All processed transcripts are dispatched strictly via Speed Post within India.

## General Tuition & Hostel Fees Status
Note: Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology does not publish public open tables for general tuition or hostel fees.
